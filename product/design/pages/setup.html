Three steps, all inside your chat app

No new app to learn. MySI plugs into the chat you already use and does the heavy lifting there.

  1. Step 1 · about 5 minutes

    Tell it what you use AI for

    Your chat app asks a few questions and turns your real work into test tasks, each with what a great answer looks like to you.

  2. Step 2 · runs on its own

    Three models answer, blind

    GPT, Gemini and Claude each answer every task. You see them only as A, B and C, so the brand can't sway you.

  3. Step 3 · a click per task

    You pick, it names a winner

    Pick the best and worst answer for each task. Then the names are revealed and MySI tells you which model to use.

Why not just check a leaderboard?

A new model ships every week, and each one claims the top spot on someone's chart. None of those charts were made from your work.

A public leaderboard

  • Scores competition math and coding puzzles
  • Graded by whoever made the test
  • One ranking for everyone on earth

Your MySI benchmark

  • Tests the text to your landlord, the lesson plan, the spreadsheet formula
  • Graded by you, blind, against what you said a great answer needs
  • A ranking for one person: you

One winner you can act on today, even if you never switch apps.

Adding it to your app

The message you copy carries these steps, so your app can walk you through them. Here they are too.

Claude: In claude.ai or the Claude app: Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Name it MySI and paste your link.

ChatGPT: On chatgpt.com, with a paid plan (Plus or higher): turn on Developer mode in Settings, then create a new app (or connector) named MySI, paste your link, and choose No authentication. In a new chat, add MySI from the + menu. When ChatGPT asks you to approve a MySI step, choose to remember it for this chat, or it will ask at every step. ChatGPT moves these menus often, so the names may differ a little.

Gemini: On gemini.google.com (US, 18 or older, personal Google account, Keep Activity on): Settings, then Connected Apps, then under Custom apps, Add a custom app. Paste your link and click Next. Then start a new chat with exactly "@MySI find my best AI model" (Gemini only uses MySI when you name it with @). When Gemini asks about a MySI step, press Always allow.
