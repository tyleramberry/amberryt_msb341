# Blind responses

Model names are hidden. The key is in blind.json; don't open it while rating.

## e1: Post-intro-call client email

**Category:** writing

**Prompt:**

Write an email to Casey, a new Space Tiger client. Tell her it was great to meet, give her the link to the payment page, and lay out the next steps.

**Context:**

We just had an intro call to learn about her and her business. The payment starts the Blueprint process. Next steps: 1) pay, 2) fill out the intake form, 3) book our Blueprint call. Payment link: [payment link]. Intake form: [intake form link]. Booking: [booking link].

**What good looks like:**

- Cordial
- Concise, nothing unnecessary
- Covers every point given
- No invented details (what we discussed, prices, dates, promises)
- The three next steps are in order and easy to scan
- Sounds like a person wrote it, not a template
- Has a short, clear subject line

### Response A

Subject: Great meeting you, Casey: next steps for your Blueprint

Hi Casey,

It was great meeting you today. Thanks for walking me through your business and where you want to take it. I'm excited to get started.

Your payment kicks off the Blueprint process. Here are the next steps:

1. **Complete your payment:** [payment link]
2. **Fill out the intake form:** [intake form link]
   It tells us about your business, your current tools, and where you're losing time. Your answers shape what we prepare for the Blueprint call, so the more detail the better.
3. **Book your Blueprint call:** [booking link]
   Choose any time that works for you. Please try to finish the intake form before the call so we can bring specific ideas instead of starting from scratch.

If you have questions along the way, just reply to this email.

Looking forward to it,

Tyler
Space Tiger
