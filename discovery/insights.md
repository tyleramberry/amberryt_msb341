**Notes on the evidence.**

- **Coverage of the four interviews.** 01 — college student, business writing, household (pilot of the
  script, not outside evidence). 02 — high school student entering freshman year; kept in the table
  on purpose: the same check-the-work behavior appears in a K-12 shape, so the college-only
  assumption in `icp.md` is something to revisit rather than a disqualifier. 03 — finance major.
  04 — accounting student. Nothing here has established what share of a real BYU student's courses
  run on Canvas rather than Learning Suite — that gap is belief 5.
- **Bets, not evidence.** Beliefs 6 and 7 come from the product-touching closing question, which the
  course treats as a condition question answered to be polite. Belief 9 rests on a single K-12
  interview and one secondhand workaround ("my friends connect their AI to Canvas").
- **Why confidence stays low.** Two of the four are not outside-ICP respondents, and the rubric-paste
  loop has two independent students behind it, not a round of them. Revise in place as the professor
  interviews and the next student round land.
