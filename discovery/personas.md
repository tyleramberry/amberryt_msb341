## The rubric self-checker

- **Who:** A college junior or senior in a business-adjacent major (finance, accounting, business
  writing) taking several courses, most or all on Canvas<sup>[01] [04] [05]</sup>. Their graded
  work is researched, cited writing: an investment memo<sup>[03]</sup>, a tax memo<sup>[04]</sup>,
  a business reply email<sup>[01]</sup>, a case analysis<sup>[05]</sup>. They use AI every week,
  and they have a firm personal rule about where it stops.
- **The problem in their words:** "Here's the rubric, here's my analysis section, am I missing
  anything?"<sup>[04]</sup> and "If it writes my memo for me, I won't touch it. That's a risk to my
  degree."<sup>[04]</sup>
- **Current workaround:**
  - Pastes the rubric and draft into ChatGPT or Claude and asks what's missing<sup>[01] [04] [05]</sup>.
  - The most technical ones have Claude pull the rubric through the Canvas API, which is "not
    reliable"<sup>[05]</sup>.
  - Rewrites any AI-assisted text back into their own voice<sup>[01] [03]</sup> and keeps Google
    Docs version history as proof of authorship<sup>[04]</sup>.
  - When they're truly unsure, asks a classmate in the same course or takes marked-up spots to
    office hours<sup>[03] [04]</sup>.
- **What they'd pay / have paid for:**
  - Most pay $0 for school tools<sup>[01]</sup>.
  - Grammarly Premium at ~$15/mo<sup>[03]</sup>; Claude Pro, which they already use for this
    job<sup>[05]</sup>.
  - Stated: $5–10/mo for "a coach that checks my work against the rubric," $15 is "think hard,"
    over $20 is a no<sup>[04]</sup>.
- **Evidence:** [01](interviews/2026-10-07-interview-01.md) (household pilot) ·
  [03](interviews/2026-10-08-interview-03.md) (BYU, mostly Learning Suite) ·
  [04](interviews/2026-10-10-interview-04.md) (UVU, Canvas) ·
  [05](interviews/2026-10-12-interview-05.md) (all Canvas; written notes, not a recording). Synthesis
  in [`insights.md`](insights.md); value proposition in [`value-prop.md`](value-prop.md).
- **How they differ:** the BYU student keeps four of six courses on Learning Suite<sup>[03]</sup>.
  Until Learning Suite is retired, that student's context comes from uploads, not Canvas
  ([decision 005](../decisions/005-learning-suite-by-upload.md)).

## Not a persona yet: the high-school student

The same check-the-work behavior appears in K-12, on paper and school-managed accounts, with the
school blocking AI and paying for tools<sup>[02]</sup>. There the district is the buyer, not the
student. Kept as a possible second segment (insight 9), not a persona, on one interview.
