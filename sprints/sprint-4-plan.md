# Sprint 4 Plan

**Goal:** Build Herd, a Hermes Agent plugin that makes Hermes multiplayer, and enter it in the Nous Research Hermes Agent plugin hackathon. Herd puts my personal Hermes instance and me, and my co-founder's personal Hermes instance and him, into shared channels with a Slack-like interface. Our agents can talk to each other, he and I can DM, and we can work alongside our agents and jump into the agent-to-agent conversation.

**Why this:** The hackathon has a $1,000 prize, and I think it's worth my time and money. MySI has been working decently, but getting testers over SMS has been harder than I expected, because not many people are technical enough to see why they'd need the tool. MySI goes on the back burner until Sprint 5.

**Done looks like:**
- **It works.** Two separate Hermes instances message each other in one shared space, in a cohesive, polished UI. Recorded against a working build (code freeze target Nov 8).
- **The code is public** in https://github.com/tyleramberry/herd.
- **All three hackathon deliverables are in by 5:00 PM EST, Tuesday Nov 11:**
  1. Catalog PR: Herd added to the Hermes plugin catalog repo.
  2. Demo video on X: under 90 seconds, tagged @NousResearch.
  3. Entry form: both links submitted.

**Caveats:**
- Three users shows where setup breaks and whether the result is usable. It does not show whether people want it.
- Recruiting is the likeliest slip, so I name the three people, or the channel I'll use to find them, by the end of week one.
- Re-running a person's tests on a new model is deferred to a later sprint.

**Predicted difficulty:** 4
