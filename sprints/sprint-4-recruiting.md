# Sprint 4 recruiting: three people outside my household

Goal from [`sprint-4-plan.md`](sprint-4-plan.md): three people who aren't family each connect MySI,
finish a run with 2 or more tests with no help, and leave with a winner. At least one uses
ChatGPT or Gemini, not Claude. Names (or the channel) are due by the end of week one.

I send these myself. Nothing here gets sent by an agent.

## Who I'm looking for

- Uses an AI chat app (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini) for real work at least a few times a week:
  school, a job, a side business. Not just "tried it once."
- Can name two things they use it for. Those become their tests.
- Is not in my household. Friends, classmates, coworkers and clients are fine.
- At least one of the three mainly uses **ChatGPT or Gemini**.
- Has 30 minutes and a laptop or desktop (setup is easiest on a computer, not a phone).

### Check before booking a ChatGPT or Gemini user

- **ChatGPT:** a custom connector needs Developer mode, which is on the web app and only on paid
  plans (Plus, Pro, Business). Ask which plan they're on. A free ChatGPT user can't connect MySI.
- **Gemini:** I haven't confirmed that the Gemini app accepts a custom remote MCP connector. If it
  doesn't, a Gemini user would have to run MySI from another app, which breaks "in the app they
  already use." Check this before recruiting a Gemini-first person, or recruit a paid ChatGPT user
  for the non-Claude slot.
- The setup page currently says "Use it in Claude" and the copied prompt says "add MySI to my
  Claude." A ChatGPT user following it as written will likely get stuck, and any help I give
  counts as an intervention. Make sure the setup page has a ChatGPT path before this session.

## Where to find them

Pick one and write it down by end of week one:

1. **Classmates** (MSB 341 and other BYU classes): people already using AI for homework and
   projects. Easiest to reach, closest to the first user's school use case.
2. **Friends or coworkers who mention AI**: anyone who's said "I asked ChatGPT…" lately.
3. **Small business owners I already know** (clients, other owners): matches the first user's
   business use case (email replies to customers).

Avoid people who will be nice to me over honest. Avoid anyone I'd have to teach what a chat app is.

## Message 1: direct text or DM

> Hey [name]! I'm building a little AI tool and I need a few people to try it
> before I show it to anyone else. It tests the AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) on the stuff
> *you* actually use AI for, and tells you which one is best for you.
>
> It takes about 30 minutes on a laptop, inside the AI app you already use. I'd just watch (screen
> share or in person) and won't help, because I'm testing whether it works without me. Then 10
> minutes of questions about what you thought.
>
> You'd want to be someone who uses ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini for school or work a few times a
> week. Would you be up for it sometime this week or next? Which app do you use most?

## Message 2: group chat or class post

> Looking for 3 people who use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for school or work. I built a tool that
> tests the AI models on your own tasks and tells you which one is best for you. 30 minutes on a
> laptop, plus a few questions after. ChatGPT users especially wanted. DM me if you're in.

## Screener (ask before booking)

1. Which AI app do you use most? Free or paid plan?
2. What are two things you've used it for in the last week?
3. Do you have a laptop or desktop you can use for 30 minutes?
4. Is it OK if I record the screen and audio? I keep the recording private and anonymize
   anything I write down.

Book them if: they use AI weekly, can name two real tasks, have a computer, and say yes to
recording. For ChatGPT users, they must be on a paid plan (see above).

## Booking confirmation

> Thanks! See you [day, time]. Bring a laptop, signed in to [their app]. I'll send you a link when
> we start. Plan for about 40 minutes total.

Don't send the MySI link ahead of time. Send it at the start of the session so setup is observed.
